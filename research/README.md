# Research Workspace

Store persistent equity research deliverables here so Antigravity agents can reference prior theses, models, comps, and sector primers across sessions.

## Layout

```text
research/
├── companies/
│   └── <TICKER>/
│       ├── thesis.md              # Living investment thesis (/thesis-tracker)
│       ├── models/                # 3-statement, DCF, comps (.xlsx + scripts)
│       ├── notes/                 # Earnings previews, post-earnings updates, channel checks
│       ├── reports/               # Initiating coverage reports (.md/.docx/.pdf) & charts
│       └── data/                  # Raw filings, transcripts, CSV exports
├── sectors/
│   └── <sector-slug>/
│       ├── overview.md            # Industry primer (/sector-overview)
│       ├── competitive-landscape.md  # Competitor matrix & moats (/competitive-analysis)
│       ├── comps/                 # Peer trading multiples (.xlsx)
│       └── decks/                 # Optional slide decks (.pptx)
├── screens/                       # Stock screens & idea shortlists (/idea-generation)
├── morning-notes/                 # Daily morning meeting notes (YYYY-MM-DD.md)
└── catalysts.md                   # Coverage-wide catalyst calendar (/catalyst-calendar)
```
